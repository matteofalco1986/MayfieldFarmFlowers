Wedding Flowers

Beautifully wild & inspired by the seasons

Creating bespoke floral designs that reflect your unique love story with flowers grown on our farm

Your Dream Wedding Flowers

Harmonising beautiful florals with a fine eye for detail, our designs are both luxurious and stylish. We believe every wedding should be as unique as the couple celebrating, which is why we work closely with you to create bespoke arrangements that tell your story.

Using flowers grown right here on our farm, we ensure the freshest blooms for your special day. Our seasonal approach means your wedding flowers will be at their absolute peak, creating arrangements that are not only stunning but sustainable and locally sourced.

Our Wedding Services

Bridal Bouquets

Ceremony Arrangements

Our Wedding Process

1

Initial Consultation

We start with a relaxed chat about your vision, venue, and style preferences. This can be in person at the farm, over the phone, or via video call.

2

Proposal & Planning

Based on our discussion, we create a detailed proposal outlining arrangements, seasonal flower suggestions, and pricing for your special day.

3

Design Development

We refine the details together, considering your venue layout, color palette, and any special requests to perfect every element.

4

Your Wedding Day

We handle all the setup and delivery, ensuring your flowers are fresh, perfectly arranged, and exactly as envisioned for your celebration.
