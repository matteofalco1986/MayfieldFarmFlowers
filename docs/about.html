About Mayfield Farm Flowers

Seasonal British flowers for homes, businesses, events and weddings

Our Story

Nestled in the heart of the beautiful English countryside, Mayfield Farm Flowers grows on fertile soil that has been lovingly tended for generations. Our farm sits in a picturesque valley where the rolling hills provide the perfect backdrop for our cutting garden approach to flower farming.

We purchased our land in 2019 with a dream of creating something special - a place where flowers could grow naturally and sustainably, reflecting the changing seasons and the beauty of our local landscape.

"I've always been passionate about growing flowers. There's something magical about nurturing a seed into a beautiful bloom that brings joy to people's lives. Every bouquet tells a story of the seasons and our connection to the land."

— Sarah, Founder & Head Grower

"After years in the corporate world, I yearned to reconnect with nature and do something meaningful. Working with the soil, watching the seasons change, and growing flowers that mark life's special moments - it's incredibly fulfilling."

— James, Co-Founder & Farm Manager

Our Growing Philosophy

We believe in the traditional "cutting garden" approach - growing flowers specifically for cutting and arranging, rather than for display in the ground. This means we can focus on varieties that not only look beautiful but also have excellent vase life and sturdy stems perfect for arrangements.

Our flowers are grown using sustainable, environmentally-friendly methods. We work with nature, not against it, using companion planting, natural pest control, and soil health practices that have been proven over generations.

What We Grow

Our flower selection changes with the seasons, ensuring you always receive the freshest, most beautiful blooms at their peak. From spring tulips and daffodils to summer dahlias and cosmos, autumn chrysanthemums to winter evergreens and berries.

Spring

Tulips, daffodils, ranunculus, sweet peas

Summer

Dahlias, cosmos, sunflowers, larkspur

Autumn

Chrysanthemums, asters, rudbeckia

Winter

Evergreens, berries, dried flowers
